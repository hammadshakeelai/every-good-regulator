# Manuscript style sheet — *Every Good Regulator*

Keep this open while drafting. It exists so every chapter sounds like the same book.

## Voice
- Clear, warm, precise. Dry humour that always carries a fact. Short paragraphs.
- Talk to the reader as "you" when giving them something to do; tell stories in the third person.
- Explain every idea as if to a smart friend outside the field. No jargon without a plain restatement in the same paragraph.
- British spelling (organisation, practised, centre, pressurised).
- Numbers: words for one to nine, numerals from 10. Percentages as numerals with "%".

## Truth rules (non-negotiable)
- Every factual claim traces to `research_notes/`. Where a fact is not there: `[VERIFY: …]` inline. Never a plausible guess.
- No invented people, quotes, scenes, dialogue or composite characters. Comic-strip characters (Pat, Dee, Unit 7) are the only fiction, and they live only in comics and clearly labelled "Dispatches".
- Direct quotes from sources: short (under 15 words), attributed. Paraphrase everything else.
- Anonymous forum posts: paraphrase only, describe as "one developer", never quote at length; handles only if permission is later obtained.
- Where an interview is needed: `[INTERVIEW: who — on what]`.
- Where the author's own experiment belongs: `[AUTHOR EXPERIMENT: …]`.
- Tell every failure as several contributing conditions, never one villain (Cook).
- Date every claim about AI tools and results.

## Chapter furniture (see FUN_TOOLKIT.md)
Markdown conventions:
- `> **THE MECHANISM** …` with `[FIGURE n.n: description]`
- `> **IN SMALL WORDS** …` — only the thousand most common English words
- `> **MYTH** … / **RECORD** … / *Source:* …`
- `> **WEIRD TRUE THING** …`
- `> **AT THE MOVIES** …`
- `[COMIC n.n: panel-by-panel description]`
- Chapter ending, in order: **The Image · The Reversal · The Rules · Diagnose · Try This · Your Turn · In One Paragraph · Go Deeper · The Haiku Line**
- Footnotes as `[^n]`.

## Humour-free zones
Chapter 7 (Post Office Horizon) entirely. The 737 MAX, Columbia and Therac-25 case sections wherever they appear. No haiku, comic, joke footnote or Myth stamp in those places.

## The five words
**Model · Legibility · Remainder · Stop · Jump** — use them consistently, introduce each once properly, never add a sixth coined term without cause.
